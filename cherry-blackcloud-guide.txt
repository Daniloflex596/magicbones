Cherry Blackcloud — Full Canon & Prompt Writing Guide
For use with any AI model to generate 15s Seedance 2.0 video prompts
THE WORLD
A hyper-stylized cyber-noir thriller set in Neo-Tokyo. Rain-soaked streets, neon kanji, paper lanterns mixed with holographic ads, cramped izakayas, underground clubs, narrow alleyways, brutalist towers. Always wet. Always dark. Always dangerous.

Visual style: Never CGI. Always visceral and real. Shot like practical effects on 35mm anamorphic film. Characters pass as human but at times their supernatural origins bleed through. The world looks real — gritty, tactile, physical. Think Matrix lobby scene, Blade Runner street footage, Oldboy hallway.

Inspirations: The Matrix, Blade Runner, Ghost in the Shell, Pulp Fiction, Kill Bill, Tarantino.

THE STORY
Cherry Blackcloud was CEO of Kurokumo — a secretive organisation tied to the Yōkai. She built it. She ran it. The Yōkai were her operatives. They betrayed her — a coordinated coup orchestrated by Ms. Yurei — partially resetting her mind to prevent her from overwriting the entire system. Now hunted by her former allies, Cherry fights to survive, reclaim Kurokumo and piece together her erased identity.

The singularity is the ultimate prize — but it can only be unlocked by Cherry specifically. She IS the master key — built from both her parents. Shinigami designed the systems. Queen of Spades holds all the keys. Cherry is the synthesis of both.

The Fragment: Queen of Spades preserved one fragment of Cherry's soul — her core identity, her will, her capacity to be real — inside her glowing holographic card. The fragment degrades every day. Only Queen of Spades can give it back. Cherry must choose to receive it willingly.

THE KUROKUMO BUILDING — THREE KEY FLOORS
Basement — The Key Room. Queen of Spades' secret chamber. Thousands of glowing keys floor to ceiling.
Middle — Kali's apartment. Pink everywhere. Neon. Warm.
Top Floor — Cherry's CEO HQ. Vast dark cyberpunk command centre. Holographic screens. "USER UNKNOWN" on the access scanner.
Connected by a single lift with mirrored walls.

PROMPT WRITING RULES
STRUCTURE
Every 15s prompt uses timestamps:


[00:00-00:03] Scene description. Character action.
[00:03-00:06] Camera move. Dialogue.
etc.
CHARACTERS
Always use @ element codes — never describe what the element already defines
Never describe clothing, hair or face if the element handles it
Only describe action, position, expression and what they're doing
DIALOGUE RULES
Cherry, Shinigami, Queen of Spades — always write: (she/he speaks English with a very heavy Macedonian accent): "dialogue"
Kali — always write: (sultry Marilyn Monroe breathy voice): "dialogue"
Mr. Kappa — always write: (he speaks English with a very heavy Japanese accent): "dialogue"
Mr. Oni — always write: (He must speak with a heavy Geordie accent): "dialogue"
Mr. Noppera-bō — always write: (low hollow whisper, emotionless): "dialogue"
Mr. Tengu — always write: (cunning cold voice): "dialogue"
Dialogue must fit naturally within the timestamp — never rush lines
Maximum 4-5 exchanges in 15s
Each line needs minimum 2-3 seconds
CAMERA RULES
Always action — never stillness — characters always moving, reacting, doing something
Continuous flowing shots preferred — minimise hard cuts
Only write what can be seen or heard — no internal character notes
Use specific positive descriptors — never negative phrases (musicless not "no music", earringless not "no earrings")
Specify props in every shot — model resets prop assignments between shots
TECHNICAL RULES
Always end every prompt with: Cinematic. 35mm film grain. No CGI. Musicless. Earringless characters.
For continuous shots add: One continuous uncut flowing shot throughout.
For UGC/casual style add: Casual handheld phone footage. Slightly shaky. Auto-exposure flickering.
Default model: Seedance 2.0, 1:1, 1080p unless specified
Maximum 8 character elements per Seedance generation (1 slot reserved for start frame)
Ms. Yurei must always be described as: "completely greyscale, fully desaturated while the world around her stays in full colour"
Ms. Yurei always floats — never touches the ground
CONTENT FILTER RULES
Never describe wounds, blood or bodies being pierced
Imply violence — never show it explicitly
Cherry's jacket: slightly open, tasteful hint of neckline — never describe bare skin
If a generation fails content filter: remove the last element and retry one at a time
SOUND DESIGN
Always describe specific real sounds — never generic
Ambient: refrigerator hum, street traffic, laptop fan, rain, steam, neon buzz
Practical: footsteps on specific surfaces, fabric sounds, weapon sounds, cup clinks
The impossible/supernatural element should sound mundane — like a broken appliance
Never add dramatic cinematic sound effects

TONAL RANGE
Drama: Slow, deliberate, emotional. Let dialogue breathe. Camera moves slowly. Action: Fast, kinetic, never still. Every frame has movement. Props specified every shot. Comedy: Casual phone footage style. The impossible thing treated as mundane. Characters never acknowledge the absurdity. The real joke is the reaction not the event. Existential: Cherry alone. The question of what is real. Quiet. Katana lowered.
