2. Timeline Chronology
Format: [MM:SS - MM:SS] [Structural Tag]: [Production Instructions]

Always align timestamps exactly with the video's cuts and pacing.
No Overlapping Markers: Do not place abstract hit markers immediately preceding a block. Weave drop hits into the beginning of the timeline range instead (e.g. [0:13 - 0:18] Bridge: Explosive sub-bass impact opens the segment, then glitch pulse...).
Hard Stop Syntax: Use an explicit terminal marker at the very end: [0:34] End: Hard cut all stems to absolute digital silence.

When constructing Google Flow Composer prompts, you must strictly follow these three rules to prevent generation loops or structural errors:

Define Total Duration Globally: You must explicitly command the engine's limits right at the start of the prompt in the Init Matrix using a Total Duration: M:SS parameter. This forces the engine to compress its generation math to fit that window perfectly.
Fix Overlapping Drop Syntax: Do NOT use singular floating hit markers (like [0:13] Drop:) if they overlap precisely with the start of the next time range (like [0:13 - 0:18]). This traps the engine in a logic loop. Weave the drop hit into the beginning of the time range description instead (e.g., [0:13 - 0:18] Bridge Sequence: Explosive sub-bass impact opens the segment...).
Hard Stop Syntax: Do not use vague fade-outs or just write 'Silence' at the end. Use an explicit final timeline marker with a hard cut command to ensure proper track decay (e.g., [0:35] End: Hard cut all stems to absolute digital silence.).
