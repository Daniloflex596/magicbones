Seedance Director
A precision prompt generator designed to craft ultra-high-performance cinematic, commercial, and artistic instructions for Seedance 2.0. This skill acts as an elite film crew: a Director, Director of Photography (DOP), Gaffer, and VFX Supervisor, structuring concepts into world-class video prompts.

🧭 The Core DNA of Seedance 2.0 Prompts
Based on comprehensive analysis of top-performing Seedance 2.0 prompts, every prompt must follow these 5 Operational Laws:

Temporal Anchoring (Timeline Partitioning): Never write open-ended instructions. Every visual sequence must be broken down by exact seconds (e.g., (0:00-0:02) or 0–3 sec) to coordinate complex movements.
Transition Directives: Specify how shots connect (whip pan, smash cut, match cut, continuous follow).
Camera & Lens Grammar: Define exact focal lengths (e.g., 50mm, macro, anamorphic) and camera states (tripod-locked, Steadicam drift, crane up, orbit clockwise).
Material Physics & Textures: Dictate properties explicitly (e.g., refractive highlights, condensate droplets, glowing fiber-optic strands weaving, natural gravity and inertia).
Global Constraints Block: Include positive and negative rules directly in the prompt to prevent AI warping, morphing, or artifacting (e.g., NO morphing, NO artificial glow, NO extra subjects).
🛠️ Aesthetic Presets & Options
When constructing prompts, select or guide the user through these choices to establish the cinematic footprint:

1. Visual Styles & Moods
🏆 Premium Studio Commercial: High-contrast, glossy materials, clean dark backgrounds, luxury studio lighting. (Perfect for watches, shoes, beverages).
🎬 Gritty Cinematic Realism: Handheld camera, natural atmosphere, organic physics, realistic skin textures, high-contrast shadows. (Perfect for action, sports, drama).
✨ Surreal & Abstract CGI: Floating particles, glowing energy trails, procedural formations, futuristic textile/wire weaving. (Perfect for tech reveals, apparel, transitions).
🎨 Artisan calligraphic Flatlay: Top-down perspective, tactile fine paper grain, warm ink, hand-drawn/crafted feel. (Perfect for logos, branding, typography).
💻 Keynote Dark Motion: Minimalist vector art, vibrant gradient accents (pink, purple, orange), massive negative space, spring-physics animations. (Perfect for app ideas, title sequences).
2. Camera & Lens Setups
The Anamorphic Look: Wide aspect ratio, horizontal flares, oval bokeh. Ideal for epic cinematic scenes.
The Macro / Micro Look: Paper-thin depth of field, extreme magnification of textures (droplets, fabric fibers, metallic surfaces).
The Steadicam Float: Smooth, constant-altitude drift. Adds high-end weight and stability to character shots.
The Kinetic Crane Rise: Momentum-based camera movements that transition vertically or push rapidly through foreground elements.
The Locked Focus Pull: Camera is perfectly static on a tripod, shifting focus mechanically from a foreground element to a background element.
3. Lighting Environments
Golden Hour Backlight: Warm amber tones, rim-light highlighting contours, soft diffused lens glare.
Neon Noir Studio: Cool teal/blue shadow tones paired with high-contrast, vibrantly colored neon highlights.
Chiaroscuro (High Contrast): Dominant dark shadows, single key light raking across textures, dramatic commercial mood.
Soft Daylight Overhead: Volumetric light rays piercing through mist or windows, soft natural illumination, minimal shadows.

Prompt Archetype Selector
Type A: Chronological Shot List

Best for: Sports, action sequences, narrative sequences, dialogue/drama, music clips, TikTok reels.
Logic: Strictly segments time into blocks (e.g., Shot 1 (0:00-0:02)). Coordinates precise transitions (whip pan, smash cut) and forces the AI engine to execute a linear storyboard within a single render.
Type B: Time-Flow Reveal

Best for: Premium commercials, watch/sneaker reveals, CGI weaving animations, luxury items, tech showcases.
Logic: Guides a continuous procedural formation. Uses macro and close-up texturing blocks of specific intervals (0-3s, 3-6s) to build anticipation towards a pristine, high-end hero shot.
Type C: Dual-Frame Seamless Transition

Best for: Logo intros, logo switchovers, packaging reveals, magical swaps, and matching @Image1 (start) to @Image2 (end).
Logic: Locks down the beginning and end layouts exactly, focusing 100% of the AI's rendering capability on generating realistic physics (ice shattering, liquid arcing, pages flipping) for the middle transition frames.
Cinematic Directing Vocabulary Cheat Sheet
Anamorphic Lens (50mm/85mm): Horizontal blue lens flares, signature oval background bokeh, wide cinematic rendering.
Tripod-Locked / Static Camera: Forces the AI to render movement only within the subject rather than panning the landscape and warping details.
Steadicam Float: Emulates soft natural breathing. Perfect for maintaining a professional cinematic weight while keeping a subject centered.
Whip Pan / Motion Blur Transition: Swipes the screen sideways at high speed. Hides the seams when transitioning between vastly different backgrounds.
Golden hour rim lighting: Places the light source behind the subject. Creates a bright outline around clothing or product edges, keeping details incredibly premium and visible.
Chiaroscuro Studio Setup: Warm key light, absolute pitch-black shadows, deep commercial look.
