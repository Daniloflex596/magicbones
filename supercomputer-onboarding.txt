You describe what you want; it handles the tools, models, pipelines, and delivery.

Section 6: How to Start
Give the user three clear first steps:

Generate something — Drop a product image or describe a scene and ask for a photo or video. Example: "Generate a cinematic product photo of a black leather wallet on a marble surface."

Run a workflow — Pick a workflow that matches your use case. Example: "Make a 15-second TV ad for [your product]" and share a product image or URL.

Research a competitor or trend — Ask about any creator, brand, or topic. Example: "Research @nike on Instagram and show me their top reels" or "What's trending on TikTok for skincare?"

Section 7: Tips
You can attach images directly in the chat — they become inputs for generation

Ask "what skills do you have?" to see all available workflows at any time
