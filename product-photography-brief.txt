Product Photography Brief Workflow
This workflow automates the planning and generation of highly detailed, production-ready product photography briefs for e-commerce brands, creative studios, and physical products. It takes a raw product name, description, category, or homepage/listing URL and outputs an actionable photography brief with exact prompts engineered for Higgsfield generation models.

Required Inputs
When invoking this workflow, the user should provide:

Product Name & Category (e.g., Organic Avocado Face Serum, Leather Minimalist Wallet, Stainless Steel Travel Mug).
Product Details (e.g., ingredients, texture, color, packaging type, labels).
Brand Aesthetic (e.g., minimalist/clean, rustic/earthy, high-end luxury, vibrant/pop, dark/moody).
Target Platforms / Aspect Ratios (e.g., Amazon, Instagram, Pinterest, Shopify).
Workflow Steps
Step 1: Input Analysis & Context Extraction
Identify and extract key attributes to anchor the visual style:

Hero Ingredient/Material: What raw inputs represent the product? (e.g., oats, leather grain, lavender).
Packaging/Labeling Text: Does the shot need legible labels? If yes, route the packaging shots to imagegen_2_0 (GPT Image 2).
Brand Colors & Palette: Warm neutrals, bright primary colors, cool blues/greens.
Lighting Archetype:
Soft Studio: Diffused, even light, soft shadows (ideal for beauty, skincare).
Hard/Chiaroscuro: High contrast, dramatic shadows (ideal for luxury, men's grooming).
Golden Hour / Natural: Warm, directional, organic shadows (ideal for wellness, food).
Step 2: Model Routing Matrix
Match each required shot type to the optimal Higgsfield model:

Shot Type	Legible Text Required?	Recommended Higgsfield Model	Rationale
Hero / Studio Packshot	No	nano_banana_pro / nano_banana_2	Outstanding studio lighting, crisp textures, high fidelity.
Macro / Texture Close-up	No	nano_banana_pro	Incredible micro-detail rendering for gels, creams, or fabrics.
Packaging / Text Detail	Yes	imagegen_2_0	Best spatial control and legibility for text characters/logos.
Lifestyle / In-Use Shot	No	text2image_soul_v2 / soul_v2	Preserves realistic human face identities and hand-holding context.
Creative / Editorial Ad	No	cinematic_studio_2_5	Highly artistic composition, dramatic lighting, luxury-campaign vibes.
Step 3: Select 4–6 Shot Archetypes
Choose the most relevant shots from the 8 Core Shot Types to form a cohesive shoot plan:

The Hero Shot: Clean product alone on a seamless background with commercial lighting.
The Macro / Detail Shot: Extremely close-up, highlighting texture, materials, ingredients, or craftsmanship.
The Lifestyle / Contextual Shot: Product in its natural environment of use (e.g., on a clean bathroom shelf).
The Flat Lay: Top-down overhead layout with curated raw ingredients, tools, or styling props.
The Unboxing / Packaging Shot: Showcases outer packaging, box texture, and clean branding.
The Hand / Human Context Shot: A hand holding, pouring, or applying the product.
The Range Shot: Multiple variants or sizes grouped together.
The Creative / Editorial Ad Banner: Dramatic lighting, conceptual art direction (e.g., bottle floating in splashing water).
Step 4: Map Platform-Specific Aspect Ratios
Apply the standard crop dimensions to the generated outputs:

Shopify / Amazon Main Image: 1:1 (square, pure white seamless background for compliance).
Instagram Feed: 4:5 (portrait crop).
Pinterest / Blog Headers: 2:3 (vertical ratio).
Instagram Reels / TikTok / YouTube Shorts: 9:16 (full screen mobile).
Website Banner: 16:9 / 21:9 (horizontal landscape).

Technical Specifications & Prompts
Negative Prompt Banks
Add these negative prompts to maintain clean, professional-grade commercial imagery:

Universal E-Commerce Negative Prompt: low resolution, draft, blurry, out of focus, distorted proportions, low quality, noise, grain, ugly, text artifact, double branding, cluttered background, cheap plastic look, artificial shadows.
Anatomical Negative Prompt (for Hand/Lifestyle shots): extra fingers, deformed hands, mutated fingers, fused digits, double hands, backward hand, claw hand, poor anatomy, unnatural pose.
Amazon Main Image Negative Prompt: shadow, text, logo, watermark, accessory, prop, colored background, off-white, shadow on product, reflection, graphic element.
Camera & Lens Specifiers (to inject in prompts)
Use precise cinematography and camera language to dictate shot rendering:

For Macro shots: Captured on 100mm macro lens, f/2.8, shallow depth of field, sharp focus on raw cream texture.
For Studio/Hero shots: Shot on 85mm prime lens, clean studio lighting, f/8 aperture, razor-sharp edge definition, commercial photography style.
For Lifestyle shots: Shot on 35mm lens, natural daylight, organic shadows, f/4 aperture, high-end editorial lifestyle photography.
Output Verification Checklist
Before presenting the final brief to the user, ensure it contains:

Strategic Analysis: Short brand alignment and aesthetic justification.
Cohesive Shot List: 4 to 6 numbered shots matching the brand's aesthetic.
Exact Model Selections: Assigned using the Model Routing Matrix.
Copy-Pasteable Prompts: Generation prompts containing specific lens specs, lighting styles, backgrounds, and negative prompts.
Multi-Platform Aspect Ratios: Clear distribution mappings.
